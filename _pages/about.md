I am a third-year undergraduate student majoring in **Bioinformatics** at Tongji University, with broad interests in **artificial intelligence, computational biology, and generative modeling**.

My research interests include **virtual cell modeling**, **AI agents**, and generative methods for scientific discovery. On the machine learning side, I am particularly interested in **diffusion models, flow matching, reinforcement learning, and large language models**, as well as how these methods can be extended to biological and other scientific problems. I am also interested in AI-driven molecular modeling and drug discovery as application areas.

Beyond domain-specific applications, I am especially interested in the development of **general-purpose AI agents** that can reason, use tools, interact with complex environments, and assist with scientific or technical workflows.

More broadly, I enjoy understanding both the mathematical foundations and the evolving methodology of modern artificial intelligence. This website also serves as a place where I organize and share my **research progress, technical explorations, and personal study notes**.

My long-term goal is to explore how AI can move beyond prediction toward **modeling, reasoning, generation, interaction, and autonomous scientific discovery**.
